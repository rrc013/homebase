Homebase Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

Homebase is a private family organizer used only by the members of one household. It is not offered to the public.

What Homebase accesses

When a parent connects a Google account, Homebase asks for permission to read the list of calendars in that account and to view, create, edit and delete events on those calendars. This lets the family's calendars appear on the Homebase screens and lets family members add events from Homebase.

How the data is used

Calendar data is used only to display and manage the family's events inside Homebase. It is never sold, shared with third parties, used for advertising, or used to train AI models. Events from calendars marked as "work" are shown to other household members only as "Work Meeting"; their details are visible only to the account owner.

Where the data is stored

Homebase stores a copy of upcoming events, along with the family's lists, notes and photos, in a private database hosted by Supabase. Access is restricted to signed-in members of the household. Google access tokens are stored server-side and are never sent to devices.

Removing access

A parent can disconnect a Google account at any time from Homebase's Family tab, which deletes that account's calendar data from Homebase and revokes access. Access can also be removed at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Google API Services User Data Policy

Homebase's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Contact

Questions: contact the household administrator who set up Homebase.
